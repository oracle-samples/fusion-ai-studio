# Purchasing Workflows

## Compliance Checklist

| **Code** | XX_COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Displays active compliance checklist items for review. |
| **Exposed to Agentic Apps** | Yes |
| **Output** | `InitDisplay` retrieves active compliance checklist items and displays them as a read-only message list. |

## My Recent Requisitions

| **Code** | XX_MY_RECENT_REQUISITIONS |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Displays the signed-in user's most recent purchase requisitions and their current statuses. |
| **Exposed to Agentic Apps** | Yes |
| **Output** | `InitDisplay` retrieves recent requisitions and displays them as read-only cards. |

## Purchase Agreement Status Distribution

| **Code** | XX_PURCHASE_AGREEMENT_STATUS_DISTRIBUTION |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Displays the distribution of purchase agreement statuses. |
| **Exposed to Agentic Apps** | Yes |
| **Output** | Retrieves purchase agreement status counts for the selected period and displays the distribution as a chart. |

## Purchase Agreements

| **Code** | XX_PURCHASE_AGREEMENTS |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Displays purchase agreement counts and details for a selected category. |
| **Exposed to Agentic Apps** | Yes |
| **Output** | `InitDisplay` displays purchase agreement counts. `InvokeAction` displays purchase agreements for the selected category. |
